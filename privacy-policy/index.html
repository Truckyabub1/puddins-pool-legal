Puddin's Pool Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Puddin's Pool is operated by Brydan William Carey.

Data Collection

The native iPhone app wrapper does not intentionally collect personal data, create accounts, collect contact information, or include analytics SDKs.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Apple handles payment information. Puddin's Pool receives purchase entitlement status needed to unlock purchased benefits such as ad removal.

Advertising

The initial native wrapper does not include a third-party advertising SDK. If advertising is added later, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Website Content

The app loads game content from https://puddins-pool.netlify.app/. Hosting logs or infrastructure records may be handled by the hosting provider according to that provider's terms and privacy practices.

Children

Puddin's Pool is a billiards game and is not directed at children for data collection. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For support or privacy requests, use the support page linked from this site.
